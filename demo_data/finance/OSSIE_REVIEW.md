## CASA transactions (`CASA_Txn`)

One accepted CASA event row per workbook business snapshot. CASA means current account and savings account. An event can recur across snapshots. Amounts are unsigned and in source currency. They are transaction flows, not deposit balances, and they cannot supply a deposit balance. The transaction description and reference are excluded from this projection.

Synonyms: CASA, current account and savings account transactions, deposit account transactions, account statement lines, payments and transfers, CASA movements. Primary key: SourceBatch, BusinessDate, TransactionID.

Names, birth date parts, sex, email, address, contact details and identification numbers are not columns: they are personal data and the identification subgroup can hold an NRIC. The Account and Entity of one position share a row by position only. The form does not state that they belong together. Join to STR_Transaction by SourceRevision to relate them to the reported transactions of the same form. Template-bound physical mapping that works only for this form type.

Synonyms: reported accounts, STR accounts, STR entity profile, suspicious transaction report account, reported entities, account and entity profile. Primary key: SourceRevision, GroupSeq.
